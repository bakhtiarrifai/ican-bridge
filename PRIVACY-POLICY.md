# Privacy Policy for iCan Bridge

**Effective Date:** August 19, 2026

## 1. Introduction

iCan Bridge is an mobile application that reads blood glucose notifications from the Sinocare iCan i3 app on your device and writes them to Google Health Connect on the same device.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and what choices you have.

## 2. Information We Collect

iCan Bridge does **not** collect, transmit, or store any personal data on our servers.

The app only accesses the following locally on your device:

- **Notifications** from the Sinocare iCan i3 app, which contain blood glucose readings.
- **Health Connect** data to write blood glucose records to your device's Health Connect store.

We do not collect your name, email, location, or any other personal information.

## 3. How We Use Information

The blood glucose data is used solely to:

- Parse the glucose value from the Sinocare iCan i3 notification.
- Write the value to Health Connect on your device.

This data remains on your device. We do not transmit, share, sell, or store your health data.

## 4. Third-Party Services

iCan Bridge uses the following services:

- **Google Health Connect**: to store health data on your device. Health Connect is provided by Google and governed by [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **Sinocare iCan i3 app**: the source of the original notification data.

We do not use analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or any other third-party SDK that collects personal data.

## 5. Data Security

Because all processing happens locally on your device, your data stays under your control. We recommend that you keep your device secure with a screen lock and keep your apps updated.

## 6. Children's Privacy

This app is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
